Leave Policy - Acme Corp

1. Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 21 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Annual leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.75 days per month of service. New employees
become eligible to take annual leave after completing 90 days of continuous employment.

Unused annual leave can be carried forward to the next calendar year, up to a maximum of
7 days. Any leave balance beyond that cap is forfeited on December 31st and does not carry
over. Carried-forward leave must be used by March 31st of the following year.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry
forward to the next year and unused sick leave is not paid out on exit.

A medical certificate from a registered physician is required for any sick leave exceeding
2 consecutive working days. Employees must notify their manager before 10:00 AM on the day
of absence, except in genuine emergencies.

3. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of fully paid leave, available to employees who have completed
at least 6 months of continuous service before the expected delivery date. This can be
extended by an additional 8 weeks of unpaid leave upon written request.

Paternity leave is 3 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's
birth or adoption date.

4. Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to 5 days of paid bereavement leave in the event of the death of an
immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling), and 2 days for extended family.

5. Leave Request Process
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in
advance for planned leave. Emergency leave may be submitted retroactively but must be
reported to the manager within 24 hours. Leave exceeding 10 consecutive working days
requires additional approval from the department head.

6. Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per year, as published annually on the HR portal
by January 15th. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, no substitute weekday is granted
unless stated otherwise in the annual holiday calendar.

7. Unpaid Leave
Employees with at least 2 years of continuous service may apply for unpaid leave of up to
30 days per year, subject to manager and HR approval. Unpaid leave exceeding 15 days in a
single instance requires department head sign-off and may affect benefits continuity.
